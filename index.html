Factorstock

The simplest stock screener for factor investing. Built with Next.js and TypeScript, Factorstock analyzes 30,000+ stocks worldwide using five academic factors and transforms financial complexity into clear, actionable scores.

  • 📊 5 academic factors: Size, Value, Quality, Momentum, Growth
  • 🌍 30,000+ stocks analyzed worldwide
  • ⚡ Instant comparison — no ratios, no noise
  • 🧠 Based on evidence-based & smart beta strategies
